# METADATA FOR StudentsPerformance Dataset

## 1. DATASET DESCRIPTION

This dataset contains data on the academic performance of students in mathematics, reading, and writing. 
It also includes demographic and socioeconomic factors such as gender, race/ethnicity, parental level of education, 
lunch type, and whether the student completed a test preparation course. 

The dataset is commonly used to analyze factors that influence student academic outcomes and to explore 
relationships between socioeconomic status, educational background, and academic performance.

---

## 2. SOURCE INFORMATION

* **Original File Name:** StudentsPerformance.csv
* **Source URL:** https://app.box.com/s/ji910ez3ycw137rw07xnhielxey7ww41
* **Format:** Comma-Separated Values (CSV)

---

## 3. COLUMN DESCRIPTIONS

This dataset contains 8 columns:

1. **gender**
   * **Description:** The gender of the student.
   * **Data Type:** Text (Categorical)
   * **Values:** "female", "male"

2. **race/ethnicity**
   * **Description:** The student's racial or ethnic background.
   * **Data Type:** Text (Categorical)
   * **Values:** "group A", "group B", "group C", "group D", "group E"

3. **parental level of education**
   * **Description:** The highest level of education attained by the student's parent(s).
   * **Data Type:** Text (Categorical, Ordinal)
   * **Values:** 
     "bachelor's degree", 
     "some college", 
     "master's degree", 
     "associate's degree", 
     "high school", 
     "some high school"

4. **lunch**
   * **Description:** The type of lunch the student receives, often associated with socioeconomic status.
   * **Data Type:** Text (Categorical)
   * **Values:** "standard", "free/reduced"

5. **test preparation course**
   * **Description:** Indicates whether the student completed a test preparation course.
   * **Data Type:** Text (Categorical)
   * **Values:** "none", "completed"

6. **math score**
   * **Description:** The student's score on the mathematics exam.
   * **Data Type:** Integer (Numerical)
   * **Range:** 0 – 100

7. **reading score**
   * **Description:** The student's score on the reading exam.
   * **Data Type:** Integer (Numerical)
   * **Range:** 0 – 100

8. **writing score**
   * **Description:** The student's score on the writing exam.
   * **Data Type:** Integer (Numerical)
   * **Range:** 0 – 100
